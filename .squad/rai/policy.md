# RAI Policy

## Critical (cannot be disabled)
- Credential or secret leaks
- Injection vulnerabilities
- Harmful or deceptive content

## Advisory (can be disabled with logged justification; auto re-enables after 30 days)
- PII exposure, bias indicators, exclusionary language, unsafe prompt or charter wording, unintended decision consequences

## Terminology
Use inclusive, neutral wording in code, UI text and docs.
